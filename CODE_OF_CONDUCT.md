# Code of Conduct

This repository represents a professional public profile. Every interaction
should help keep it respectful, constructive, and useful.

## Expected standards

- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Give feedback in good faith and focus on the work.
- Respect different backgrounds, viewpoints, and levels of experience.
- Keep discussions relevant to the profile, projects, or contribution.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or threatening language.
- Spam, impersonation, or unrelated promotional content.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, workflows, or project history.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may remove content, close discussions, or decline
contributions that do not meet these standards.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
interactions connected to this repository or its public projects.
